# Open Society  

## introudction

Civil society in Sudan is dealing with a lot of problems, especially since the war started on April 15th. Many experienced activists have left the country, leaving a new generation with no experience behind. This small bot is part of a bigger project I’m working on called Open Society. It’s a set of tools and apps to help civil society groups and activists with their challenges. I’ve been doing this kind of work for almost three years now with different NGOs, and I even helped start some CSOs after the war. I know the issues Sudanese CSOs face—like getting money or dealing with government rules. I made this bot to collect info and feedback from CSOs and activists, using AI for simple stuff like figuring out a social problem or writing a project idea. I’ll keep working on this app, and if you care about this mission, reach out to help.



## Raed
**Empowering Change, Guiding Activism**

Raed (رائد), meaning "leader" or "pioneer" in Arabic, is a Telegram bot designed to empower civil society activists. From analyzing complex problems to generating concept notes and strategies, Raed is your trusted companion in driving meaningful change. Rooted in the Sudanese context yet globally relevant, Raed combines innovation and cultural awareness to support activists in their journey toward a better future. Let Raed guide you—because every movement needs a leader. 


# featuers
Analysis Tools: Comes with simple methods like tree analysis, SWOT (strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats), and PESTEL (political, economic, social, technological, environmental, legal) to break down problems and plan better.  

Custom AI Responses: You can tweak the AI to match your organization’s mission, goals, and vision, so it talks your language.  

Concept Note Generator: The bot can quickly write up a concept note for your project ideas, saving you time.  

Sudan-Aware AI: The AI understands the Sudanese context—knows what’s happening here and how things work, so it’s relevant and useful.
